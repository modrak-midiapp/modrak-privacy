MODRAK Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

1. Scope

This policy describes how the MODRAK application (the "App") handles information. It applies to the App on all platforms on which it is distributed.

2. Developer and contact

The App is published by MODRAK. Questions about this policy may be sent to modrak.support@gmail.com.

3. Accounts, analytics and advertising

The App does not require an account and does not ask you to sign in. It contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting software. MODRAK does not collect, sell or share personal data.

4. Network requests

The App sends network requests only when you choose to do so, and only over HTTPS:

(a) Refreshing or searching the MIDI profile list retrieves the list from api.github.com.

(b) Downloading a MIDI profile retrieves a device parameter file (CSV) from raw.githubusercontent.com.

These requests are received by GitHub in the same way as any web server, and GitHub may record the IP address and time of each request. No other data is transmitted. No usage data, device identifier or project content is sent.

5. Data stored on your device

The App stores the following information in its own storage on the device: projects, pages, scenes, saved modules and folders, copies of MIDI profiles, application settings, and your plan (Free or Pro) as last confirmed by Google Play. This information is not uploaded. Uninstalling the App deletes it. Exporting a project copies its text to the clipboard; any further use of that text is at your discretion.

6. Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. Google Play handles payment and holds the purchase record. MODRAK does not receive payment card details or your Google Play account information. The App asks Google Play whether the current account owns Pro and stores the result on the device. If the store cannot be reached, the stored plan is not changed. Refunds and purchase history are managed by Google Play.

7. Permissions

The App requests internet access only, for the network requests described in section 4.

8. Email

If you write to modrak.support@gmail.com, MODRAK receives your email address and message and uses them only to reply. The App opens a draft in your mail application; it does not send email on its own.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from any person.

10. Your choices

You may delete projects and MIDI profiles within the App, or uninstall the App to remove all stored data. To request deletion of email you have sent to MODRAK, contact modrak.support@gmail.com.

11. Changes to this policy

MODRAK may update this policy. The updated version will be published at this address and will show a new effective date.
