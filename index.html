MODRAK privacy policy

Effective 2026-11-18

MODRAK makes this app. Contact: modrak.support@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There is no account. You do not sign in.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting software.
  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

What leaves your device

The app makes two downloads over https, and only when you ask:

  1. The MIDI profile list, from api.github.com, when you refresh or search it.
  2. A device's parameter file (CSV), from raw.githubusercontent.com, when you download a MIDI profile.

GitHub sees these requests the way any web server does, including your IP address and the time.

Nothing else is sent. No usage data, no device identifier, no project content.

What stays on your device

Projects, pages, scenes, saved modules and folders, your MIDI profile copies, settings, and your plan (Free or Pro) as Google Play last confirmed it. This is stored in the app's own storage and is not uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes it. Export copies a project's text to the clipboard; where it goes next is your choice.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Google Play takes the payment and holds the receipt. We do not see your card or your Play account. The app asks Play whether this account owns Pro and saves that answer on the device. A store that cannot be reached changes nothing. Refunds and purchase history are handled by Google Play.

Permissions

Internet: for the two downloads above.

Email

If you write to modrak.support@gmail.com, we see your address and your message and use them only to answer. The app opens a mail draft; it does not send mail itself.

Children

The app is not made for children and collects nothing from anyone.

Your choices

  • Delete what you made: delete projects and MIDI profiles in the app, or uninstall the app.
  • Ask a question, or ask us to delete mail you sent: modrak.support@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published with a new date at the same address.
